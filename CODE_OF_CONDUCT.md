# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Mentingo is an open project maintained by [Selleo](https://selleo.com). Everyone taking part - contributors, maintainers, people opening issues or asking questions in Discussions - is entitled to a harassment-free experience, regardless of experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including in disagreement.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback, and give it with enough context to act on.
- Respect that maintainers decide what belongs in the project, and that "no" is a legitimate answer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and pull requests.
- Posting details of an unfixed security vulnerability in public - see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces - the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions - and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@selleo.com**. <!-- ETAP 2: potwierdzić skrzynkę i osobę odpowiedzialną -->

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review, respond within 5 business days, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban a participant depending on severity. Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith may face the same consequences.
