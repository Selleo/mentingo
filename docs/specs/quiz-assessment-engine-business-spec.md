# Quiz Assessment Engine Business Spec

## Business Overview

The Quiz Assessment Engine lets course authors add scored checks inside Mentingo courses. It gives learners a structured way to answer questions, submit an assessment, see their score, and retake the quiz when the course rules allow it.

For HR and L&D teams, quizzes make learning completion more meaningful. Instead of tracking only whether a learner opened a lesson, teams can require a passing score, control retake attempts, and use quiz completion as part of course progression and certificate workflows.

The core workflow starts in curriculum authoring, where a creator adds a quiz lesson and configures questions plus quiz settings. Learners later answer the quiz in the lesson player, submit their answers, and progress only when the quiz rules are satisfied.

## Who Uses It

- Course authors create quiz lessons and add question types that match the learning objective.
- L&D administrators configure passing thresholds, attempt limits, and cooldowns for assessment governance.
- Learners submit answers, review scores, and retake quizzes when allowed.
- Reviewers grade open answers (brief and detailed responses) that wait for manual review.
- Training managers use quiz outcomes as evidence that learners understood required material.

## Feature Functions

- Create quiz lessons inside the curriculum builder.
- Add single choice, multiple choice, true/false, photo choice, fill-in-the-blanks, brief response, and detailed response questions.
- Reorder questions and edit quiz translations while locking structural edits in non-base languages and showing base-language text as an authoring reference.
- Configure passing threshold, optional attempt limit, and optional retake cooldown.
- Require learners to answer every quiz question before submission.
- Score learner submissions and store question results.
- Let authors mark brief and detailed responses as "Graded by reviewer"; without it they keep counting as correct.
- Hold quizzes with reviewer-graded open answers in a "Waiting for review" state until a reviewer grades them.
- Give reviewers a "To grade" queue with filters and a grading view where they mark each answer as pass or fail and leave a comment; grading the last open answer finalizes the score and completes the lesson when passed.
- Show learners their final score, the points for each reviewer-graded question, and the reviewer's comment.
- Show feedback after submission, including correct-answer context for failed gap-filling questions when feedback is enabled.
- Hide answer-level feedback after submission, retakes, and privileged lesson views when course quiz feedback is disabled.
- Mark the lesson complete only when the learner passes the quiz.
- Let learners retake eligible quizzes and show why retake is unavailable when attempts or cooldown rules block it.

## End-User Value

Quizzes help organizations confirm knowledge, not just content exposure. HR and L&D teams can add lightweight assessments to compliance, onboarding, or skills training, while learners get clear feedback about their score and whether another attempt is available.

## How It Works

A course author adds a quiz lesson in the curriculum builder, gives it a title, chooses question types, fills in answer options or response fields, and configures assessment rules. If the course is translated, Mentingo allows translation edits while preventing non-base-language structural changes such as adding or reordering questions. Empty translated quiz fields show their base-language text as a placeholder; structured gap-fill sentences show a separate read-only base-language preview so authors can translate them without changing the underlying blank structure.

A learner opens the quiz lesson, answers every rendered question, and submits the form. Mentingo validates that no expected question is missing, evaluates the answers, calculates the percentage score, and compares the result with the passing threshold. Passing submissions complete the lesson and can unlock course progression; failed submissions keep progression rules in place.

After submission, learners can review which answers were accepted when quiz feedback is enabled for the course. Answer content follows the selected course content language, falling back to the course base language when unavailable. The UI supplies localized feedback headings; the API returns answer content without a “Correct answer” prefix. Fill-in-the-blanks questions show the correct completed sentence for failed answers, so learners can understand the intended wording instead of seeing only the words they chose. When course feedback is disabled, Mentingo still records the score and submitted answer but keeps answer-level correction details hidden, including after a learner starts and submits a retake or when an admin opens the lesson view.

Brief and detailed responses count as correct whenever an answer is submitted, unless the author switches on "Graded by reviewer" for that question in the quiz editor. Existing open questions were kept on the always-correct behaviour, so reviewer grading only applies where an author opts in. When a submission contains reviewer-graded open answers, Mentingo grades the automatic questions right away but saves each open answer as pending with 0 points and keeps the whole attempt pending, without a score, result, or graded date. The lesson is not completed, no quiz-completed or lesson-completed event is emitted, and the learner sees "Waiting for review" on the quiz, in the course outline, and in the lesson sidebar. While waiting, the learner sees their own submitted answers and a partial result: automatically graded questions show their right or wrong marks and a count of correct graded questions, while reviewer-graded answers show "Waiting for review" and no score is shown. When quiz feedback is disabled for the course, the partial result is hidden as well. The learner cannot resubmit or retake the quiz. The pending attempt still counts toward the attempt limit and cooldown. Learner statistics and course quiz results leave pending attempts out, and certificates cannot be issued because the lesson stays incomplete. If the learner repeats the course, the pending attempt stays with the earlier run. Admins in learning or support mode and freemium learners follow the same rule. Attempts already graded are not re-graded when an author changes a question's review setting. Quizzes without reviewer-graded open questions are graded immediately as before.

Reviewers (and admins) open the "To grade" queue, which lists the organization's reviewer-graded answers, oldest first, with a pending counter, filters by course, lesson, learner, and status (pending by default, graded answers can be shown for re-grading), and pagination. Reviewers never see or grade their own answers. A reviewer lands on the queue after signing in. The grading view shows the question, the learner's answer, the author's reviewer instructions when present, and the grading history. The reviewer marks the answer as pass (the question's full points, 1 point today) or fail (0 points) and can add a comment for the learner. Pass/fail is the only grading method for now; the grade request names its method and each grade stores it, so others can be added later and past grades keep their verdict even if the question's points change. "Next answer" moves straight to the next pending answer. When the last pending answer of an attempt is graded, Mentingo calculates the score and pass/fail result, records the quiz on the learner's progress, completes the lesson if the quiz is passed, and emits the quiz-completed and quiz-attempt-graded events, all in one transaction. The learner then sees the final score, the points for each reviewer-graded question, and the reviewer's comment. Re-grading an answer adds a new revision and keeps the history; it recalculates the attempt score and, when it is the learner's latest attempt, their quiz score on the lesson; it can complete a lesson that now passes, but never reopens a lesson that was already completed. Answers from a run the learner has since repeated drop out of the queue and cannot be graded. The same goes for answers from a course switched back to draft or from a learner who is no longer enrolled; the reviewer gets a clear message instead, and the answers come back once the course is published or the learner re-enrolled.

When a learner retakes a quiz, Mentingo first checks the attempt limit and cooldown. If retake is allowed, previous answers are cleared and a new attempt begins. If not, the retake button remains disabled and the interface explains the remaining limit or cooldown.

## Key Technical Context

- Learner quiz delivery lives in `apps/web/app/modules/Courses/Lesson/Quiz.tsx` and the question components under `apps/web/app/modules/Courses/Lesson/Question`.
- Quiz authoring lives in `apps/web/app/modules/Admin/EditCourse/CourseLessons/NewLesson/QuizLessonForm`.
- The translated quiz editor loads the selected and base-language course variants through the existing localized course query. Questions are paired by their shared identity, while language-specific answer rows are paired by structural position.
- Quiz evaluation and retake orchestration are implemented in `apps/api/src/lesson/services/lesson.service.ts`; target-schema delivery and scoring live in `apps/api/src/quiz/services/quiz-runtime.service.ts`. Dedicated services coordinate quiz authoring and attempt persistence; repositories execute all database reads, writes, and transactions. Attempt writes participate in the caller’s transaction when one is supplied.
- Gap-filling result feedback is built from the localized target assessment definition and the learner's latest target attempt, including the submitted text and accepted answers.
- Passing quiz completion publishes a `QuizCompletedEvent` through the outbox and updates student lesson progress. A submission awaiting review publishes no events at all (no quiz-completed, lesson-completed, chapter or course events, so no gamification or reviewer notification yet); grading by a reviewer completes it.
- Open questions use grading mode `participation` (always correct) by default and `manual` when the editor sends `requiresReview: true`; migration `0235_keep_open_questions_participation_graded` moved existing open questions from `manual` to `participation`. Questions with grading mode `manual` make the attempt pending; "awaiting review" is derived from a pending attempt in the learner's active learning state (`apps/api/src/quiz/quiz-awaiting-review.sql.ts`), not stored on lesson progress.
- Reviewer grading lives in `apps/api/src/quiz-grading` (`/api/quiz-grading/answers`, `/filters`, `/answers/:id`, `/answers/:id/grade`), protected by `quiz_grading.read` and `quiz_grading.grade`. Each grade is a revision in `assessment_attempt_question_answer_reviews`. Finalizing reuses the same progress, lesson-completion and `QuizCompletedEvent` path as automatic grading (`StudentLessonProgressService.finishQuizProgress`), runs in the attempt's learning state, and also publishes `QuizAttemptGradedEvent` (also on re-grades), which nothing consumes yet; reviewer notifications (#2125) will.
- SCORM exports grade in the learner's browser without a reviewer, so open answers there count as participation: any non-empty answer earns full points.
- Match-words and scale question types exist in code paths but are not exposed in the current question selector.

## Test Evidence

Frontend E2E coverage verifies quiz authoring for choice, text, fill-in-the-blanks, and photo questions; confirms unavailable question types in the builder; verifies learner submission with every rendered question type, both with open answers counted as correct and with reviewer-graded open answers ending in "Waiting for review"; covers quiz retake, final-quiz retake after certificate completion, blocked progression after failing a required quiz, visible text gap-fill correction when feedback is enabled, and hidden gap-fill correction after retake when feedback is disabled. Unit coverage verifies base-language placeholder matching, including duplicate option display orders and readable gap-fill previews, as well as fallback correct-sentence rendering for completed fill-in-the-blanks questions. Backend E2E coverage verifies feedback redaction for learners, admins, and course authors when the course setting disables feedback. It also covers translated authoring and feedback, base-language fallback, preserving answer identities after translated edits, and rolling back a submitted attempt with its caller’s transaction. Open-answer review coverage verifies the pending attempt and answers, incomplete lesson progress, no quiz-completed event, statistics ignoring the pending attempt, the awaiting-review lesson and course status with a partial result (graded questions only), the partial result hidden when quiz feedback is disabled, blocked resubmission and retake, and unchanged immediate grading for quizzes without manual questions. Grading coverage (`quiz-grading.controller.e2e-spec.ts`) verifies the queue, filters and grading view, finalizing the attempt with lesson completion, both events and the learner's points and comments, re-grade history without reopening the lesson, a re-grade that turns a fail into a pass, mixed automatic and reviewer-graded quizzes, refusing automatically graded and own answers, a repeated course run, draft courses and unenrolled learners, fractional points with the stored grading method, two last answers graded at the same time finalizing the attempt once, rejecting grades without a supported method, permission checks, and tenant isolation; a Playwright test verifies that grading the last answer completes the learner's lesson. The curriculum language E2E test proves that translated content remains editable while base-language structure is protected, but a browser-level assertion for the new placeholder presentation remains to be added.
